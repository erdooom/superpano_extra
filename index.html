SuperPano — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

SuperPano (“the app”) is a photo-stitching app. This policy explains what it accesses and what happens to your data.

Summary

SuperPano processes your photos on your device and does not have its own servers. The app does not upload your photos or sell your personal data. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, whose SDK collects device/usage data to serve them (see “Advertising” below); the paid ad-free subscription removes ads and their data collection.

What the app accesses

  • Camera — only while you use in-app capture, to take the photos you choose to shoot.
  • Photos you select — to stitch them into a panorama. Images are read from the locations you pick and processed locally.
  • Device motion sensors (accelerometer / rotation vector) — to power the on-screen level, coverage map, and to tag each shot’s orientation for better stitching. This stays on the device.
  • Storage (save to gallery) — when you tap Save, the finished panorama is written to your gallery (Pictures/SuperPano) via Android’s scoped storage. The app does not request broad file-system access.

What leaves the device

  • Your photos never leave automatically. A panorama leaves the device only when you choose to share it (via the Android share sheet, to an app you pick) or save it to your own gallery.
  • Purchases: the one-time “Pro” unlock and the ad-removal subscription are processed by Google Play Billing. The app does not see or store your payment details; Google handles the transaction and tells the app only whether the item is owned. See Google’s own privacy policy.

Advertising (free version)

  • The free version shows banner ads via Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device and usage data (for example a device advertising identifier, IP address, and app interactions), as described in Google’s advertising/privacy policies.
  • Where required (e.g. the EEA/UK), a consent prompt (Google’s User Messaging Platform) is shown before personalized ads are used.
  • Buying Pro is separate from ads. The ad-free subscription (or a granted license) removes the banner and its ad requests entirely.
  • Your photos are never used for advertising.

Data collection and sharing

  • SuperPano itself collects no personal information and contains no analytics SDK. The only third-party data collection is by Google AdMob in the free version (above) and Google Play Billing for purchases.
  • SuperPano does not sell your personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children. SuperPano itself collects no personal data; the free version’s ads are served by Google AdMob under Google’s policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change and the new version will be posted at the same URL.
